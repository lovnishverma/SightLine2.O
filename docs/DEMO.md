# 3-Minute Hackathon Judge Demo Script
**Build With Bharat 3.0 — Chitkara University, Himachal Pradesh**
**Team SightLine Intelligence**

---

## Demo Overview
This script is engineered for a seamless, high-impact 3-minute live demonstration in front of hackathon judges. It highlights SightLine's transformation:
$$\text{SEE} \longrightarrow \text{UNDERSTAND} \longrightarrow \text{RESEARCH} \longrightarrow \text{VERIFY} \longrightarrow \text{ACT}$$

---

## Pre-Demo Setup
1. Start the application:
   ```bash
   python app.py
   ```
2. Open `http://localhost:7860` in the browser.
3. Ensure webcam is connected or keep sample images handy in an accessible folder.
4. Verify audio output is unmuted.

---

## 3-Minute Presentation Walkthrough

### STEP 1 — Local Visual Perception (0:00 – 0:30)
- **Action:** Select mode `Quick Glance` or press `D` while facing a scene (or presenting an appliance).
- **Say to Judges:**
  > *"Most assistive visual tools describe what's in front of a user. Here, Florence-2 perceives the scene immediately with high-speed local inference."*
- **SightLine Speaks:**
  > *"I see a Bosch washing machine in the center of the frame."*
- **Show Judges on Screen:**
  - Status indicator displays `● Local Vision Engine`.
  - Latency is under 1.5 seconds.

---

### STEP 2 — The Shift to Contextual Intelligence (0:30 – 1:00)
- **Action:** Select mode `Intelligence` and ask: *"Tell me more about this."* (or click the quick inquiry chip **⚙️ How to Use?**).
- **Say to Judges:**
  > *"SightLine's IntelligenceRouter detects that 'Tell me more' is not a simple color or counting query—it requires real-world technical knowledge. It autonomously routes to the official iNSIGHTS DeepSearch engine."*
- **SightLine Speaks:**
  > *"This appears to be a Bosch Series 6 front-load washing machine. It features a 15-minute quick wash program and an EcoSilence motor. Turn the dial to Super 15 and press Start to begin."*

---

### STEP 3 — Evidence & Verification Panel (1:00 – 1:30)
- **Action:** Point the judges to the **iNSIGHTS INTELLIGENCE** panel below the description box.
- **Say to Judges:**
  > *"We never present hallucinated AI claims as facts to visually impaired users. Every single statement is backed by verifiable sources retrieved through iNSIGHTS."*
- **Show Judges on Screen:**
  - Status badge: `● iNSIGHTS DeepSearch Active`.
  - **Sources Found:** Real manufacturer links (e.g. `media3.bosch-home.com` user manual).
  - **Verified Knowledge Context:** Specifications, wash programs, and control dial details.

---

### STEP 4 — Conversational Follow-Up Without Re-Querying (1:30 – 2:00)
- **Action:** Type or ask: *"What does error E18 mean?"* (or click **📖 Find Manual**).
- **Say to Judges:**
  > *"Notice that we did not re-upload an image or re-explain the appliance. SightLine's bounded Knowledge Cluster remembers the active entity and provides immediate answers from previous research."*
- **SightLine Speaks:**
  > *"For this Bosch machine, error E18 indicates that the drain pump filter is blocked or the waste water hose is kinked."*

---

### STEP 5 — Document Intelligence & Deadlines (2:00 – 2:30)
- **Action:** Switch mode to `Read Document` and upload/show a university scholarship notice or circular.
- **Say to Judges:**
  > *"Blind students cannot independently read notice boards or circulars. SightLine doesn't just read raw OCR—it extracts deadlines, eligibility, and required actions."*
- **SightLine Speaks:**
  > *"This notice announces a merit scholarship application. The deadline is September 20th. Required documents include your marksheet, identity proof, and bank details."*
- **Show Judges on Screen:**
  - Structured deadline card: `September 20, 2026`.
  - Required documents bullet points.

---

### STEP 6 — Hyper-Local Hindi Voice Adaptation (2:30 – 2:50)
- **Action:** Click **🇮🇳 Explain in Hindi** (or switch voice to `Hindi - Swara` and ask: *"Isko kaise use kare?"*).
- **Say to Judges:**
  > *"For true nationwide impact across Bharat, SightLine adapts the research and speaks in natural Hindi via Indic neural TTS."*
- **SightLine Speaks (Hindi):**
  > *"यह बॉश वाशिंग मशीन है। शुरू करने के लिए मुख्य डायल को सुपर 15 पर घुमाएं और स्टार्ट बटन दबाएं।"*

---

### STEP 7 — Accessibility & Closing (2:50 – 3:00)
- **Action:** Press `Esc` to interrupt speech (demonstrating hands-free tactile keyboard control `D`, `R`, `P`, `Esc`).
- **Say to Judges:**
  > *"SightLine is accessibility-first, evidence-backed, and powered by official iNSIGHTS intelligence. It turns passive sight into independent action. Thank you!"*
